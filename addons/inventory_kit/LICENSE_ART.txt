Inventory Kit - art in this repository (Asset Library edition)

The images in addons/inventory_kit/art/ are PLACEHOLDERS: coloured shapes with the item
initials, drawn by a script, same names and sizes as the real art. They are under the MIT
license, like the code (see LICENSE.txt), and the item databases, skins and themes work
with them unchanged.

The hand-made art (120 items in 4 art directions: painted, realistic, cartoon, flat, with
matching UI frames) is free, pay what you want from $0, on the official page:
https://banc-essai.site-testeurs.workers.dev/inventory-kit/
Drop its addons/inventory_kit/art/ folder over this one: same file names, nothing to rewire.
That art has its own license (free to use in your games, not to be re-uploaded on its own).
